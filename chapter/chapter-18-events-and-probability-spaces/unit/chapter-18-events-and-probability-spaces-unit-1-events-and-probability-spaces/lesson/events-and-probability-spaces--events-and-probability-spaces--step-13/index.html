Problem. From a standard deck (consisting of 52 cards), six cards are drawn. Find the probability that among the six cards not all four suits are present.

As an answer, you may enter either a fraction (for example, \(\texttt{2/7}\)) or a mathematical expression (for example, \(\texttt{1 - binomial(13, 2) / binomial(52, 6)}\)).
