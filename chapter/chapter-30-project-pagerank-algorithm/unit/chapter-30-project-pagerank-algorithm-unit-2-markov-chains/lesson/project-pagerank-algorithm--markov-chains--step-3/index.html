Problem. Consider the following random process: at each step, a knight makes a random move on a chessboard. Let \(\alpha_{i}\) be the position of the knight after \(i\) steps. Is \(\{\alpha_{i}\}_{i=0}^{\infty}\) a Markov chain?

Question
