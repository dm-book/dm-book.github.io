Colorings · Colorings and Degree

Lesson 1

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

As we remember, \(\chi(G) \le \Delta(G) + 1\). Again, it's natural to ask: if a graph requires few colors to color, does it mean its degree is small? It is easy to see that this is not the case: for example, a star graph can have arbitrarily large degree, but the graph itself can be colored with two colors.
