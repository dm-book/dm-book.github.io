Colorings · Theory Problems

Lesson 2

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

Advanced Problems.

  1. (20 points) In a graph \(G\), out of any three simple cycles of odd length, at least two have a common vertex. Prove that \(\chi(G) \le 8\).
  2. (20 points) Let \(F=(V,E_{F})\) and \(G=(V,E_{G})\) be graphs with the same set of vertices \(V\). Let \(H = (V,E_{F} \cup E_{G})\).Construct a function \(f\) of two arguments, such that for any such \(F,G,H\):\[\chi(H) \leq f(\chi(F),\chi(G)).\] Moreover, show that for any \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) there are graphs \(F, G\) on at least \(n\) vertices such that \(\chi(H) = f(\chi(F),\chi(G))\).
  3. (20 points) Let \(k\) be the maximum number of edges in a simple path in \(G\). Prove that \(\chi(G)\le k+1\).
    Hint:
    Find a node with degree at most \(k\)
  4. (20 points) Let \(G\) be a graph on \(n\) vertices and \(\overline{G}\) be its complement. Prove that:(a) \(\chi(\overline{G})\chi(G) \ge n\).(b) \(\chi(\overline{G}) + \chi(G) \le n + 1\).
    Hint:
    (b) Try induction on \(n\).
  5. (20 points) Prove that for any graph \(G\) the multiplicity of root 0 of the chromatic polynomial \(P_{G}(x)\) is exactly equal to the number of connected components of \(G\).
    Hint:
    Use the deletion–contraction formula and induction on the number of edges.
