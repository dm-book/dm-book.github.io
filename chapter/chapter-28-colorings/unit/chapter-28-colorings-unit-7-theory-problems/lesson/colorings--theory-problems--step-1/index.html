Colorings · Theory Problems

Lesson 1

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

Basic Problems.

  1. (5 points) Let \(\chi(G - \{u, v\}) = \chi(G) - 2\) for any two distinct vertices \(u, v\) (removing vertices \(u, v\), not an edge). Prove that \(G\) is a complete graph.
  2. (5 points) Prove that, for any graph \(G\), the number \(0\) is a root of \(P_{G}(x)\) with multiplicity at least equal to the number of connected components of \(G\).
  3. (5 points) Prove that, for a graph \(G([n], E)\) with degree sequence \(d_{1} \geq d_{2} \geq … \geq d_{n}\),\[\chi(G) \leq \max_{i} \left(\min \{d_{i} + 1, i\}\right).\]
  4. (5 points) Prove that, for any graph \(G\), there is a proper \((\Delta(G)^{2} + 1)\)-coloring such that the graph distance between any two vertices of the same color is at least three.
  5. (5 points) Find the chromatic polynomial of a complete graph and a tree.
  6. (10 points) Prove that, for any graph \(G\), the coefficients of \(P_{G}\) are integers and their signs alternate.
  7. (10 points) Prove that, for any \(G(V, E)\), \([x^{|V| - 1}] P_{G}(x) = -|E|\).
  8. (10 points) Prove that any graph \(G\) with \(\chi(G) = k\) has at least \(k\) vertices whose degree is at least \(k-1\).
  9. (10 points) Let \(G\) be a directed graph whose outdegrees of vertices do not exceed \(d\). Prove that \[\chi(G) \le 2d + 1.\] Here, the chromatic number of a directed graph is defined as the chromatic number of the corresponding undirected graph obtained by ignoring the orientation of the edges.
  10. (10 points) Show that the edges of the complete graph \(K_{n}\) can be colored with 3 colors such that the number of monochromatic triangles is at most \(\frac{1}{9}\binom{n}{3}\).
  11. (15 points) For a graph \(G([n], E)\), \[P_{G}(3)=3\cdot 2^{\ n-1}.\] Prove that \(G\) is a tree.
  12. (15 points) Let \(G\) be a graph obtained from a convex \(n\)-gon by triangulating it with non-intersecting diagonals (that is, the vertices of \(G\) are the vertices of the polygon, and the edges are the sides of the polygon together with the drawn diagonals). Find its chromatic polynomial \(P_{G}(x)\).
  13. (15 points) For a graph \(G\), \[P_{G}(x) = x^{6} - 7x^{5} + a x^{4} - 17x^{3} + b x^{2}.\] Find \(a\) and \(b\).
  14. (15 points) Prove that any graph \(G\) has at least \(\binom{ \chi(G) }{ 2 }\) edges.
  15. (15 points) Prove that, for any interval graph \(G\), \(\chi(G)=\omega(G)\).
  16. (15 points) Prove that, for any connected \(G([n], E)\), \[(-1)^{n} \cdot P_{G}(x) < 0, \quad \text{for }0 < x < 1.\]
