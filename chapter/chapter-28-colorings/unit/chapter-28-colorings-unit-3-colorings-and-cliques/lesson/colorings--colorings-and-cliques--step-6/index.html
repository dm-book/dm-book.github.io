How many edges can a graph without large cliques have? This question is answered by Turán's theorem, of which Mantel’s theorem is a special case. Informally, Turán's theorem says the following: to have no \((r+1)\)-cliques in a graph and still have as many edges as possible, you should divide the vertices into \(r\) approximately equal parts and consider the complete \(r\)-partite graph.
