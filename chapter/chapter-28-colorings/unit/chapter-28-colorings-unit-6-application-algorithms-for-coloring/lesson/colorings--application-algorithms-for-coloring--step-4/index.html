To find a coloring, one can proceed as follows: sequentially add edges to the graph that do not increase its chromatic number; eventually, a complete \(k\)-partite graph will be obtained, which is easy to color with \(k\) colors.

Constructing an algorithm with runtime \(O^{*}(1.99^{n})\) for computing the chromatic number remains an open problem. Finding the chromatic number in \(O^{*}(2^{n})\) time and polynomial space is also an open problem.
