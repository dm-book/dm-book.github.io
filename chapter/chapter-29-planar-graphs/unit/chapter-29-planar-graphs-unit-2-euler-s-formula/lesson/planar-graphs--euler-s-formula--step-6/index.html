Theorem (Pick). The area of a simple polygon with vertices on the grid \(\mathbb{Z}^{2}\) is \[n_{\operatorname{int}}+\frac{n_{\operatorname{bd}}}{2}-1 \ ,\] where \(n_{\operatorname{int}}, n_{\operatorname{bd}}\) are the number of grid points inside and on the boundary of the polygon, respectively.For example, the area of the polygon shown below is \(\textcolor{#ff5349}{4}+{\frac{\textcolor{myblue}{10}}{2}}-1=8\).
