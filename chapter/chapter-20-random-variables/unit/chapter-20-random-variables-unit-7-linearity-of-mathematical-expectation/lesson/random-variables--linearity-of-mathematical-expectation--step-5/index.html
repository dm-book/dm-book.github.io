Random Variables · Linearity of Mathematical Expectation

Lesson 5

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

Using the linearity of expectation, we can immediately conclude that, on average, the sum of two dice rolls is seven. There's no need to consider all 36 possible outcomes, compute the sum for each, and then average them. It's enough to recall that the expected value for one die is \(3.5\).
