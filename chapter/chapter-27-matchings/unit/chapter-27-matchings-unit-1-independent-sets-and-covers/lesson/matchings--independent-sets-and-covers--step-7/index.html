Problem. Check your intuition. Which of the (in)equalities below hold for any graph \(G(V,E)\) that has no vertices of degree zero?

Multiple choice
This problem can only be submitted at Cogniterra.
  1. 1

    \(\alpha(G) \le \alpha'(G)\)

  2. 2

    \(\alpha(G) \le \beta'(G)\)

  3. 3

    \(\alpha'(G) \le \beta'(G)\)

  4. 4

    \(\alpha'(G) \le \beta(G)\)

  5. 5

    \(\alpha(G)+\beta(G)=|V|\)

  6. 6

    \(\alpha'(G)+\beta'(G)=|V|\)
