Project: Optimal Circuit Synthesis with SAT · Searching Small Circuits

Lesson 6

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

The number of Boolean functions grows so rapidly with the number of variables, that makes exhaustive search too slow even for functions on \(5\) variables.

A SAT solver gives a more general way to search for a circuit and certify that a smaller one does not exist.

Problem. How many Boolean functions on \(n\) variables are there? Give your answer as a formula in \(n\).

This problem can only be submitted at Cogniterra.
