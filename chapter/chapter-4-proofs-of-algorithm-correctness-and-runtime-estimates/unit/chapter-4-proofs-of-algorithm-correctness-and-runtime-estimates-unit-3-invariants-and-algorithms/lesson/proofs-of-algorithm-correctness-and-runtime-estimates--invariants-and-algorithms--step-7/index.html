Problem. In a small town, there are two soccer teams, and each resident supports one of them. Every day the following happens: if there is a resident, among whose friends there are more supporters of the opposite team (than those supporting his team), then he also starts supporting the opposite team. Can this process continue indefinitely? (Assume that each day exactly one person starts supporting the other team and that neither the population nor the social connections change.)

In the example below, there are seven people: residents 1 and 5 support the dark team, all others support the light team. Two people are friends if they are connected by a segment: for example, residents 3 and 7 are friends, but residents 2 and 6 are not. On the first day, resident 6 starts supporting the dark team (since both his friends, residents 1 and 5, support the dark team). The next day, resident 1 starts supporting the light team, since she has three friends (2, 4, and 7), but only one of them (resident 6) supports the same team. The process ends there.

But can the process continue indefinitely for some other population?
