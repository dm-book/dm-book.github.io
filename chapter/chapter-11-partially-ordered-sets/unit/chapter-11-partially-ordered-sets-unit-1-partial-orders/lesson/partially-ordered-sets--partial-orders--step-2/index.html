Problem. Mark all sets from which it is possible to choose a decreasing sequence of infinite length (that is, a sequence \((x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, \dotsc)\), for which \(x_{1}>x_{2}>x_{3}> \dotsb\)).

Multiple choice
This problem can only be submitted at Cogniterra.
  1. 1

    \(\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\) (non-negative integers)

  2. 2

    \(\mathbb{Z}\) (integers)

  3. 3

    \(\mathbb{P}\) (primes; recall that they are positive by definition)

  4. 4

    \(\mathbb{Q}\) (rationals)

  5. 5

    \(\mathbb{R}\) (reals)

  6. 6

    \([2, 3]\) (\([2, 3]=\{x \in \mathbb{R}\colon 2 \le x \le 3\}\))
