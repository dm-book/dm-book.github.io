Lesson 1

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

Dilworth's theorem might seem like an abstract result about posets, but it's actually a powerful tool for solving problems in combinatorics. By cleverly defining a poset on the elements of a problem, we can transform it into a question about chains and antichains. This often makes the solution surprisingly elegant. Let's see this magic in action by proving three famous theorems.
