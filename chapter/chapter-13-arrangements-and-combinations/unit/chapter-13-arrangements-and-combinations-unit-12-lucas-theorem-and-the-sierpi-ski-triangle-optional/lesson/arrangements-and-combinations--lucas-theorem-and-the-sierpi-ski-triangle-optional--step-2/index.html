Lesson 2

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

The Sierpiński triangle is one of the simplest self-similar fractals. Start with a filled equilateral triangle. Divide it into four congruent smaller triangles and remove the middle one. Then repeat the same operation in each of the three remaining triangles, and so on.
