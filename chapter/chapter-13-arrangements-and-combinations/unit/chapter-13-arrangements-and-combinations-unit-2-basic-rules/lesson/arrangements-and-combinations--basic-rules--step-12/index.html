Lesson 12

Nikolai Chukhin · Alexander S. Kulikov

Bijection rule: two sets that have a bijection between them have the same size. This is another simple-to-state rule that we will use very often. We will see both simple and complex applications of it.

This rule will allow us to count many things in a straightforward way: first, we will learn to count the number of basic objects (arrangements and combinations), and then we will try to reduce everything to these basic objects.
