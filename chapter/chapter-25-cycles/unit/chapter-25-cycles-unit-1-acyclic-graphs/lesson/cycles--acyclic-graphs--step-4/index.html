At this point, it is natural to ask:

For what graphs does a topological ordering exist?

As we discussed above, a cycle in a graph is a clear obstacle. Thus, our question can be restated as follows: are there other obstacles?

We show below that no other obstacle exists! In the proof, we use a notion of a sink. In a directed graph, a sink is a node with no outgoing edges.
