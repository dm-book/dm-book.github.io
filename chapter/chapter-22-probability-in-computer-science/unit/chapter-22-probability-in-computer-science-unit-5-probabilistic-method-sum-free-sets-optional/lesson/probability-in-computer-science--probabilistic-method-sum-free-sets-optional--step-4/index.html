Programming problem. Given a set \(S \subseteq \{0, 1, \dotsc, 10^{6}\}\) of size \(100\), find its sum-free subset of size \(34\).

The set \(S\) is given in the first (and only) line of the input. Output space separated \(34\) indices (zero-based) of the subset elements in the increasing order.
